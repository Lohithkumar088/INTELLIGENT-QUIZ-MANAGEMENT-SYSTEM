Help & Support

Have a question or need assistance with your quizzes? Explore our common FAQs below or reach out directly to our team.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I start a new quiz?

Navigate to Categories from the top menu, choose your preferred subject, select your desired difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard), and tap Start Quiz.

How do I review past quiz answers?

Visit the History page to see all your completed attempts. Tap the Review button on any quiz card to inspect questions, your answers, and AI explanations.

How do AI Explanations work?

When reviewing any question you answered incorrectly, tap Generate Explanation to receive instant step-by-step reasoning generated by the AI knowledge engine.

How are Ranks, XP, and Badges calculated?

Every completed quiz awards XP based on your score percentage. Higher scores and continuous 28-day activity unlock badges like Consistent Learner and High Performer on your profile.

How do I reset my password?

You can change your password directly from your Profile Settings, or use the Forgot Password link on the login screen.
